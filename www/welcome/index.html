Built around how a day actually goes

Not a grid of 9-to-5 boxes. Not a list that only grows. Four things Cadence does that most calendar and to-do apps don't.

The whole 24 hours

The Today view is a full day, midnight to midnight — not a business-hours window. Routines repeat on their own; drag a block up, down, or to another day and it reschedules itself.

Tasks that rank themselves

Every task is scored by importance and urgency, so the top of the list is always the right thing to do next. "Fill today's free time" drops the highest-scored tasks straight into your open gaps.

Goals that don't go quiet

Goals break down into milestones that roll up into real progress — not a guess. Check-ins keep the long ones honest, and a goal that's gone two weeks without movement says so.

The truth, once a week

The weekly review compares what you planned against what you confirmed actually happened, category by category — the gap most calendars never show you.

One account, every device. Sign in on the web or Android and your day follows you — changes sync in the background, and everything still works when you lose signal.
